

Real Estate Salesforce Package – Website Brief
Prepared by: Vishakha Kumari | Version 1.0
1. Purpose of This Document
This brief tells the developer and designer what the website should look like and how
visitors should experience it. It is written in plain language and does not prescribe
technology; it describes pages, content, behaviour and the feel of the site.
The website promotes a Salesforce package built for real estate companies. The
package helps them manage their leads, sales and bookings in one place. The website has
one main job: explain the package's features and benefits clearly and persuade visitors
to request a demo.
2. About the Package
The package runs on Salesforce and gives real estate companies a ready-made way to run
their sales operation. The website presents it through three core areas, plus supporting
capabilities.

3. Business Goals
1. Generate demo requests. This is the primary goal, and every page should lead towards
it.
2. Build trust and credibility with real estate companies and the Salesforce teams that
support them.
3. Explain the features and benefits so a visitor understands the value in under two
minutes.
4. Send visitors to the AppExchange listing where relevant.
5. Give the sales team clean, complete lead information from every demo request.
How we will measure success: number of demo requests per month, percentage of
visitors who submit the demo form, time spent on the Features page, visits that come
from the AppExchange, and how many visitors come back.

5. Overall Look and Feel
Style: Modern, clean, professional and trustworthy, in the style of good business
software. Plenty of white space; not cluttered.
Mood: Confident, helpful and straightforward. It should feel like a tool that makes a
sales team's day easier.
Colours: Use the company brand colours: one primary colour, one bright accent colour
reserved for the "Request a Demo" button, and neutral greys and white elsewhere. The
brand colours and logo come from the business team (see Section 11).
Product screenshots: The product is the hero. Show clean, real screenshots of the
leads, sales and bookings screens in simple device frames, with sample data that looks
realistic and contains no real customer information.
Photos: Real, high-quality images of homes, buildings and real estate sales teams at
work. Avoid obviously fake stock photos.
Fonts: One clean, easy-to-read font family for headings and body text. Large headings,
comfortable body text size.
Buttons: The main "Request a Demo" button must look the same everywhere, stand
out from the rest of the page and use the accent colour.
Icons: Simple, consistent line icons for features and benefits.
Motion: Light, subtle animations only (gentle fade-ins, hover effects). Nothing that
slows the page or distracts.
Salesforce name and logo: Use of the Salesforce name, logo or AppExchange badge
must follow Salesforce's brand guidelines.

8. Page-by-Page Description
8.1 Home Page
Goal: Make a strong first impression and send visitors to the demo form.
Sections from top to bottom:
1. Hero banner: A big headline stating the main promise (for example, "Manage every
lead, sale and booking in one place, on Salesforce"), a short supporting sentence, and
two buttons: Request a Demo (main) and See Features (secondary). Beside or behind
the text, a large product screenshot or a short product video.
2. Trust strip: Logos of customers or partners, a "Built on Salesforce" mark, and a link to
the AppExchange listing. Headline numbers may be added once the business team
provides them.
3. Three core areas: Three large cards for Lead Management, Sales Management and
Booking Management, each with an icon, a one-line description and a screenshot. Each
card links to its section of the Features page.
4. Benefits section: Three or four big benefits (for example, never lose a lead, close deals
faster, see every booking at a glance), with a simple picture for each.
5. From enquiry to booking: A simple visual that follows one customer through the
package: enquiry arrives, sales rep follows up, deal progresses, unit is booked.
6. How it works: A simple four-step visual (for example, 1. Book a demo, 2. We set it up for
your team, 3. Your team is trained, 4. Go live).
7. Testimonials: Two or three customer quotes with name, photo and company.
8. Final call to action: A colourful band with a short message and the Request a Demo
button.

8.2 Features Page
Goal: Show exactly what the package can do, in the order a real estate sales team works.
A short introduction at the top.
One clear section for each area: Lead Management, Sales Management, Booking
Management, and Reports and Dashboards.
For each feature: an icon or screenshot, a short title, a two-to-three line description,
and a "what this means for you" sentence.
A sticky side menu or tabs so visitors can jump between the areas.
A "Works on Salesforce" section covering mobile access and how the package fits with
the rest of a company's Salesforce.
A Request a Demo button after every area.
8.3 Benefits Page
Goal: Explain the value in business terms, not product terms.
Organise by role (sales head, sales executive, owner or director, Salesforce admin).
Each benefit has a bold headline, a short explanation and, where possible, a number or
proof (for example, "Faster lead response"). Figures come from the business team.
A simple "Before vs After" comparison showing life with spreadsheets and scattered
tools against life with the package.
A savings calculator where the visitor enters a few numbers (sales team size, leads per
month) and sees an estimate of time saved and deals recovered.
8.4 Who It Is For Page
Short sections for real estate developers and builders, brokerages and agencies, and
sales and management teams.
Each section describes the typical daily challenge and how the package solves it, with
a matching screenshot and a Request a Demo button.
8.5 How It Works Page
A visual step-by-step journey from "Request a demo" to "Go live".
Estimated time for each step.
Short explanation of installation, set-up, training, data migration from spreadsheets or
other tools, and ongoing support.

8.6 Security and Salesforce Fit Page
This page is mainly for Salesforce admins and IT teams.
Plain-language explanation of how the package works with a company's Salesforce,
and where the data is kept.
How access is controlled and how data is protected.

8.7 Pricing Page
Either two to three plan cards (name, who it is for, key features, price or "Contact us")

A short list of pricing questions and answers.
8.8 Customer Stories
Cards with customer photo, company, quote and one key result.
Click a card to read a full short case study (problem, solution, result).
Optional customer video.

10. Experience Requirements
10.1 Mobile and Tablet
Many visitors will use phones, so the site must look and work well on mobile, tablet and
desktop.
Buttons and links must be big enough to tap easily.
Forms must be easy to fill on a phone (right keyboard for email, phone and so on).
Product screenshots must stay readable on small screens.
10.2 Speed
Pages should feel quick to open. Images and videos must be sized and compressed
properly.
Show a simple loading indicator if something takes time.
10.3 Easy to Use and Accessible
Text must have enough contrast against the background and be readable by people
with low vision.
All images need descriptions for screen readers.
The site must be usable with a keyboard only.
Clear, jargon-free wording; explain Salesforce terms where they appear.
Visitors should reach the demo form within one or two clicks from any page.

10.4 Trust and Security
Secure connection (padlock) on all pages.
Privacy policy and consent visible near every form.
Personal data from forms stored safely and visible only to authorised staff.
10.5 Findability (Search Engines)
Each page should have its own clear title and short description for Google.
Clean, readable page addresses (for example, /features).
Content written so that people searching for real estate CRM, lead management or
booking management on Salesforce can find the site.
10.6 Tracking and Reporting
Track page visits, button clicks, demo form submissions and visits from the
AppExchange so the business can see what is working.
Show a simple report of demo requests per week and month to the business team.
10.7 Easy Content Updates
The business team should be able to change text, screenshots, testimonials, resources
and FAQs themselves without calling a developer.
Demo requests should be viewable in a simple list that can be exported to Excel.
11. Content Needed From the Business Team
The developer cannot start the final design without these:
Product name, company logo and brand colours
Final list of package features with short descriptions, grouped by Lead, Sales and
Booking Management and Reports
Screenshots or a demo video of the package, using sample data
List of benefits and any proof points (numbers, results)
AppExchange listing link and any ratings or badges that can be shown
Supported Salesforce editions, installation steps and security wording
Customer logos, testimonials and case studies (with permission)
Team photos and company story

12. Out of Scope for Version 1
Public property search or listings for buyers and renters (this website promotes the
package only)
Customer login area or self-service portal
Online payments or online purchase of the package
Changes to the Salesforce package itself
Any of these can be added in a later phase.
13. Acceptance Checklist (How We Know It Is Done)
All pages in Section 6 are built and linked in the menu and footer.
"Request a Demo" button is visible on every page and works.
Demo form validates entries, shows the thank-you message, sends the confirmation
email to the visitor and a notification to the sales team.
Product screenshots for Lead, Sales and Booking Management are shown on the Home
and Features pages.
Site looks correct and is easy to use on phone, tablet and desktop.
Pages open quickly and images are clear.
Brand colours, logo and fonts are applied consistently.
Salesforce and AppExchange names, logos and badges follow Salesforce's brand
guidelines.
Content can be edited by the business team without a developer.
Cookie banner, Privacy Policy and Terms are in place.
Tracking of visits and demo requests is working.
Business team has reviewed and signed off on every page.
